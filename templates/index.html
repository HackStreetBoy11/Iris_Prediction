How to Measure
Sepal Length
Measure from the base of the sepal to its tip, along the center line.
Sepal Width
Measure across the widest point of the sepal, perpendicular to its length.
Petal Length
Measure from the petal base to tip. Petals are the inner, often colourful parts.
Petal Width
Measure across the widest point of the inner petal, perpendicular to its length.
